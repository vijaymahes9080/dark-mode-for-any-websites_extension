Why Dark Mode is Crucial for Visual Ergonomics

Vijay Mahesh • June 2, 2026
Media Safe Image Inversion

In our highly digitized world, we spend an average of 7+ hours looking at screens daily. The harsh glow of white backgrounds emitted by default website themes can cause significant eye strain, dry eyes, and headaches.

"Transitioning to a high-contrast dark theme reduces screen luminescence, balances ambient contrast, and lessens cognitive fatigue during late-night browsing sessions."

Universal Dark Mode works instantly on any website. Unlike extensions that simple invert every color blindly—which turns photos into creepy negatives and ruins colors—our advanced CSS engine targets text, background, and structures, while leaving images, videos, and canvas elements visually natural.
